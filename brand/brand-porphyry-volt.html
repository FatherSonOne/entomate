Design System Rationale
Hue Separation
Entomate sits at Hue 56° (Electric Volt) — far from Pulse (270° purple) and Logos Vision (220° blue). Maximum luminance against cool porphyry dark.
Tone Distinction
Deep Porphyry (purple-black) provides dramatic contrast for volt. Electric Rose signals urgency; Sky Cyan signals AI depth.
Shared Thread
All three brands use dark backgrounds, share similar shadow depths, and complement each other in a unified product ecosystem.
