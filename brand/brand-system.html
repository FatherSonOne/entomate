03 — Ecosystem

Brand Harmony

Entomate sits within the same product family as Pulse and Logos Vision. Each occupies a distinct color territory while maintaining visual cohesion.

Pulse
#8B5CF6 #06B6D4 #EC4899
Communication · Voice · Purple/Cyan
Logos Vision
#1E40AF #3B82F6 #F97316
CRM · Relationships · Navy/Blue
Entomate
#C8FF32 #8B8BFF #FF4A1C
Intelligence · Automation · Lime/Periwinkle
Design System Rationale
Hue Separation
Entomate sits at Hue 80° (Acid Lime) — 190° from Pulse (270° purple) and 140° from Logos Vision (220° blue). Maximum perceptual distance.
Tone Distinction
Warm Graphite (near-black with zero blue cast) vs. Pulse's cool-dark and Logos Vision's navy. Acid Lime pops on dark with electric clarity.
Shared Thread
All three brands use dark backgrounds, share similar shadow depths, and complement each other in a unified product ecosystem.
