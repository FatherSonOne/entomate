Design System Rationale
Hue Separation
Entomate sits at Hue 40° (Rich Gold) — far from Pulse (270° purple) and Logos Vision (220° blue). Warm authority against cool technical peers.
Tone Distinction
Deep Obsidian (cool blue-black) provides premium contrast for gold. Electric Cyan signals clarity; Violet signals AI depth.
Shared Thread
All three brands use dark backgrounds, share similar shadow depths, and complement each other in a unified product ecosystem.
