## 💡 KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

### 1. Stick to the Timeline
- **Don't add features** during Phase 1 (save for Phase 2)
- **Don't skip testing** (causes bugs)
- **Don't rush deployment** (causes outages)

### 2. Prioritize Communication
- **Daily standup** (15 mins - non-negotiable)
- **Weekly demos** (team sees progress)
- **Clear blockers** (escalate immediately)

### 3. Start Small
- **First meeting**: You record it
- **Second week**: Team records theirs
- **Third week**: CRM integration begins
- **Expand gradually**: Don't try to do everything at once

### 4. Measure Everything
- **Track metrics** from day 1
- **Report weekly** to leadership
- **Celebrate wins** (both big and small)
- **Address issues** immediately

### 5. Get User Feedback
- **Weekly feedback sessions** (15 mins)
- **Listen to team** (they know what's needed)
- **Implement quick wins** (builds momentum)
- **Plan Phase 2** based on feedback
