# Meeting Intelligence Profiles — The Vision

## The Problem Nobody's Solving

Every meeting tool on the market treats meetings the same. Record, transcribe, summarize, extract action items. Done.

But a grant proposal review with a foundation officer is nothing like a Monday standup with your dev team. A board meeting demands formal minutes and tracked resolutions. A sales discovery call needs to catch buying signals and objections. A vendor negotiation should track concessions and commitments.

The output should be different because the *purpose* is different. And yet every AI meeting tool gives you the same generic summary, the same generic action items, the same generic analysis — regardless of what the meeting actually was.

**The intelligence isn't in the transcription. It's in knowing what to listen for.**

## The Vision

Imagine this:

You schedule a meeting called "Q3 Grant Review — Ford Foundation." Before the meeting even starts, your AI already knows:

- This is a grant meeting. It pulls up the Grant Specialist profile.
- It reaches into your CRM and finds every contact from the Ford Foundation — their roles, your history with them, past meeting notes.
- It checks your project management tool for open deliverables tied to this grant.
- It surfaces the last three meetings you had about this grant — what was decided, what action items are still open.
- It pulls recent team conversations about the grant from your messaging platform.

All of this happens *automatically*, assembled into a pre-meeting intelligence briefing.

Then when the meeting runs, the AI isn't just transcribing. It's listening through the lens of a grant specialist — tracking compliance mentions, budget discussions, timeline commitments, deliverable changes, reporting requirements. Things a generic summarizer would gloss over.

When the meeting ends, you don't get a generic summary. You get:

- **Grant Status Update** — where things stand right now
- **Compliance Items** — anything mentioned about reporting or audits
- **Budget Impact** — financial discussions and implications
- **Deliverable Tracker** — what's on track, what changed, what's at risk
- **Action Items** — not just "follow up with Sarah" but specifically grant-contextualized next steps with deadlines tied to the grant timeline

And all of that feeds back into the system. The next time you meet with the Ford Foundation, the context is even richer. Every meeting compounds.

## Why This Is Different

**1. It's not a feature — it's an intelligence layer.**

Most meeting tools bolt on AI as an afterthought. MIP makes AI the foundation. The profile system doesn't just change what the summary looks like — it changes what the AI pays attention to, what it prioritizes, how it thinks about the conversation.

**2. It's ecosystem-native.**

This isn't an isolated meeting tool pulling from nothing. It reaches across your entire business infrastructure — CRM for who, messaging for conversations, meetings for history — and assembles a living picture of every relationship and project. The three apps together create intelligence none of them could alone.

**3. It learns.**

Profiles that users love get suggested more confidently. Profiles that get dismissed learn to step back. Post-meeting feedback tunes the system. Custom profiles let users build specializations for their exact workflow. Over time, the system gets better at knowing what you need before you ask.

**4. Nobody else does this.**

There are meeting transcription tools. There are CRM tools. There are project management tools. But nobody is doing pre-meeting contextual AI specialization that pulls from your own ecosystem data to shape how the AI processes a meeting. This is genuinely new.
